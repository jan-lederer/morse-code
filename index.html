Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

About this policy

This policy covers the morse Morse code learning app for Android and the web, as well as this privacy policy page. The app is maintained by Jan Lederer.

Information the app stores

morse does not require an account. Your practice progress, preferences, and other app state are stored on your device: in private app storage on Android and in your browser's local storage on the web. The app does not send this information to the developer, and it is not synced by the app between devices.

This information remains until you clear the app or browser data. Android backup and restore behavior may also depend on your device settings.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services, and it does not collect or transmit your practice content to the developer. Morse audio and vibration are generated by your device or browser when you use those features.

Network requests and hosting

The Android app works offline. The web app loads its files from the website hosting it and requests a small same-site version file to display the app version. That request does not contain your practice data.

This privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. For details about GitHub's handling of visitor information, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Permissions and choices

Android may use vibration when you enable or use vibration feedback. You can turn feedback off in the app's settings. You can clear locally stored app data in your device or browser settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app publisher through the store listing or project page from which you obtained morse.
